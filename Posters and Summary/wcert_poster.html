The Problem & The Solution

The Problem

  • Cybercrimes against women suffer severe under-reporting due to fear, stigma, and awareness gaps, and lack of jurisdictional awareness.
  • Traditional mechanisms are slow, intimidating, and lack forensic intelligence

The Solution

  • A secure, anonymous, and jurisdiction-aware reporting platform.
  • Bypasses intimidation and provides forensic integrity
  • ZERO-TRUST DATA VAULT IMPLEMENTATION: aes-128-cbc encrypting to isolate and encrypting mathematically threat and mapping to legal relevant victim PII

Forensic Escalation Routing

  • SHA-256 Chain of Custody: All evidence is hashed at upload. Append-only audit logs track every view, download, and verification to ensure legal admissibility.
  • Geo-IP Routing: The system silently captures victim state-level location upon submission even, deallocating upon and/or submission.
  • Dynamic LEW Enforcement Escalation: When an analyst clicks "Escalate", W-CERT automatically injects the exact contact details (Nodal Emails & Landlines) for the specific State's Cyber Crime Cell and Women's Commission.
